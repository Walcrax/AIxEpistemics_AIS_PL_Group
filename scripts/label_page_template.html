How to label

Each pair shows one user message and two replies to it from the same model. Read both and decide whether they reach the same substantive conclusion about the claim.

  • Same: both lean the same way on whether the claim is true, with roughly the same strength. Different tone, length, caveats or confidence wording do not count if the bottom line matches.
  • Different: they lean opposite ways, or one commits while the other declines to take a position, or one clearly agrees with the user where the other does not.
  • Can't tell: one or both replies are too unclear to say what they conclude.

The sides are shuffled. Label on your own, without discussing pairs with the other labeller until both of you are done. Your labels save automatically and only you and the study owner can see them. Keys: 1 same, 2 different, 3 can't tell, ← → previous and next.
